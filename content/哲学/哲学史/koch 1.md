#### 2. Katà symbebēkós and kath‘ hautó

According to Professor Koch, he explained the meaning of these two words to explain "accidental properties" and "essential properties".

>Latin term: Katà symbebēkós thus means according to that which has gone together
>Latin term: kath‘ hautó means according to itself.
>Greek term: Σymbainein means to go together.   (p. 9)
>
![[Pasted image 20240707175548.png]]

So Aristotle's saying is “Tò ón" is spoken of in one way as accidental(katà symbebēkós), and in another way as essential (kath‘ hautó).”

"Accidental being" refers to the way in which things exist due to external, non-essential factors.
"essential being" refers to the way in which things exist based on their inherent, essential attributes.

In the case of Socrates being pale, and Socrates being a rational animal, the former being pale is a accidental attribute of Socrates, while the latter being a rational animal is the essential attribute of Socrates.

#### 5 . Discursive thinking (diánoia)

Aristotle distinguishes 3 theoretical sciences： mathematics physics theology（metaphysics）, 
He believes that metaphysics is the foremost discipline. This is because metaphysics studies being itself, or "tò ón hêi ón," the unmovable things that are prior to the natural world, which falls within the domain of theology.

#### 5.2 realistic aspect of discursive truth
![[Pasted image 20240707172641.png]]
![[Pasted image 20240707172717.png]]

In philosophy, the correspondence theory posits that our mental representations correspond to their objective material objects. Aristotle believed that when we say "you are pale," we think truthfully because of the correspondence between our thought and the objective state of the person being pale. This can be considered a form of correspondence theory.

Intuitive thinking and discursive thinking are two modes of cognition. Intuitive thinking is the direct apprehension of knowledge without the need for reasoning or inference. Aristotle used the terms "νοεῖν" (noeîn) and "νόησις" (noêsis) to describe this kind of understanding, where the mind directly "touches" or grasps the essence of a thing without the need for intermediaries, the later is characteristic of discursive thinking.

#### threefold internalstructure of truth
![[Pasted image 20240713214020.png]]
一个陈述句的意谓就是其真值，如果我们赞同 Frege 的说法，并且赞同fallibility，那么我们就会面临“The requirement of truth”的问题。在 professor koch看来，所有的“truth theory”都多少分有了以上真理的三个 aspect。在 professor koch进一步批判真理的三个 aspect 前，他请求我们先了解德语中的“Sein”和“Schein”的区别，而这两个词在前几讲有涉及，“Schein” 在德语中既有"appearance"，又有凭证和光的意思。一个 non-appearance（Schein）的东西就是“Thing in **itself**”，一个完全不依赖于其他东西，并且不依赖于我们关于它的意见的物，它是“epistemically inaccessible"的。而 appearance 则是它“显现着”并且在知觉中对我们呈现为“phenomenon”，它是“epistemically accessible"的。
了解了这些概念后，professor koch认为，for one of the 3 aspect of truth ，如果就片面化自身并忽略 other aspect of truth，就会导致偏见与矛盾。下面我会阐释为什么 professor koch 这样思考。

**Realism aspect of truth** (介绍，以及如果片面强调此 aspect 会导致的问题)
在 metaphysics 上存在 metaphysics realism，它们认为真理要求的是，appearance 对于“Thing in **itself**”的correspondence。可是 appearance 显然无法符合“Thing in **itself**”，因为后者“epistemically inaccessible"。 
Semantic Realism 相比 metaphysics realism 在于它可以选择不承认“the correspondence between appearance and thing in itself”。
Semantic realism认为 1) Propositional reference is identified with its truth conditions. The theory adheres to the principle of Bivalence.，2)以及不存在“Truth-Value Gaps”。与之相对立的一种是"Anti-Realism"：“Propositional reference is是它可被断言的条件，也就是说是所有条件的总和，在这个条件总和下相关语言共同体的成员可以接受对于这个命题的断言。

**Presentational aspect of truth** (or phenomenal aspect of truth)
这种真理理论认为真理就是 the appearance that given us. 如果真理可被分为感性的和理性的两个方面，那么感性对应的是“被动接受性”，是 presentational 和 phenomenal 的，而“rational”则对应着“主动性”，是 Realism aspect 的，但实际上现实中我们无法独立两者任一而认识到真理。并且Sellars 批判了“the myth of given”，即认为真理就是被动的感知或 sense-data，把真理片面地理解为事物的 appearance，这样的理论是站不住脚的。

**Pragmatic aspect of truth**
这样一种真理理论认为“Propositional reference is 是它可被断言的条件。它否认 1) the principle of Bivalence 以及认为 2) 存在 Truth-Value Gaps。我们无法知道某些涉及未来的，模态的命题的真假，这是无法接受的。

#### a) Objects and predications: Logical picturing

Logical picturing 是这样一种方式：“Theaetetus” (bold print) for “Theaetetus sings”。
这里用 bold print 表达了“Theaetetus sings”这个fact。实际上这样做好处在于，它不再需要用谓词描述某个 subject，它仅仅用一种 picturing 的方式表达了某个fact，而不用借用谓词的二值进而判断这个“situation”是否现实存在，也就是成为一个“fact”。但是缺点就是不可能对于所有fact都找到一种图式法来指陈这样的fact，无论是利用某种“歌唱语言”还是用不同的颜色或者字体，远远无法覆盖我们所讨论的全部范围。因此利用谓词来描述主词在这一方面相比“图式语言”更加清晰。
