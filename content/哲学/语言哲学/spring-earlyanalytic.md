1 reductio ad absurd
**Proof by Contradiction Example: Proving that √2 is Irrational**

We use proof by contradiction to show that the square root of 2 is irrational.

Assume, to the contrary, that √2 is rational. Then, it can be expressed as a fraction a/b in lowest terms, where a and b are integers with no common factors other than 1, and b ≠ 0.

Thus, √2 = a/b.

Squaring both sides gives 2 = a²/b², so a² = 2b².

This implies that a² is even, and therefore a must be even (since the square of an odd number is odd).

So, a = 2k for some integer k.

Substituting into the equation: (2k)² = 2b² → 4k² = 2b² → 2k² = b².

This shows that b² is even, and hence b is even.

But if both a and b are even, then they share a common factor of 2. This contradicts our initial assumption that a and b have no common factors other than 1.

Therefore, our assumption that √2 is rational must be false. Hence, √2 is irrational.



2 begging the question
### 英文版（English Version）

**Example of Begging the Question: Proving Free Will Exists**  
**Argument:**  
_"Free will must exist because humans have the ability to make free choices."_

**Analysis:**

1. **Premise:** Humans have the ability to make free choices.
    
2. **Conclusion:** Therefore, free will exists.
    

**Why is this begging the question?**

- The premise _"humans have the ability to make free choices"_ **presupposes the conclusion** that free will exists.
    
- "Making free choices" is synonymous with exercising free will.
    
- The argument **assumes what it claims to prove** (i.e., "free choices" = "free will exists"), offering no independent evidence.
    
- **Circular structure:**  
    _"Free will exists because free choices exist"_ → _"Free choices exist because free will exists."_
    

**Key Mistake:**  
The premise and conclusion are logically equivalent, making the argument vacuously circular.

**私人语言论证的核心思想（Core Thesis）**

维特根斯坦认为：**不可能存在一种本质上私有、无法被公共验证的语言**。所谓“私人语言”指：

- 词汇仅指向个人私有感觉（如疼痛），且**无法通过公共标准被他人理解**。
    
- 其意义完全依赖个人内心定义，**脱离语言共同体的使用规则**。
    

> 维特根斯坦的批判：  
> **“私有对象（如‘甲虫’或‘疼痛’）在语言游戏中毫无地位”**。语言的意义在于**公共使用规则**（language-game），而非私有指称。

- **英文结论**：
    
    > A "private language" is impossible because:  
    > (1) Words gain meaning only through **public rule-governed use** (language-games).  
    > (2) Private objects (beetles/pains) are **epiphenomenal to meaning**—they play no role in determining how words function.  
    > (3) Any attempt to name private sensations (e.g., "S" for my pain) collapses into circularity, as I cannot establish rules for "S" without public criteria.
    
- **中文结论**：
    
    > 私有语言不可能存在，因为：  
    > (1) 词语意义仅源于**公共规则下的使用**（语言游戏）。  
    > (2) 私有对象（甲虫/疼痛）**与意义无关**——它们不参与决定词语功能。  
    > (3) 命名私有感觉（如用“S”指代我的疼痛）会导致**循环定义**：脱离公共标准，我无法为“S”建立规则（例如：何时算“相同疼痛”？）。

_What would it be like if human beings showed no outward signs of pain? Then it would be impossible to teach a child the word ‘toothache’.”_  
**（“若人类无疼痛的外在表现，我们如何教会孩子‘牙疼’一词？”）**

> Ostensive definition is the act of pointing to an object while uttering a word (e.g., pointing to an apple and saying "apple").

- **Early Wittgenstein (Tractatus)**:
    
    > Simple objects **can only be named**, and ostensive definitions anchor language to reality by connecting names to primitive entities.  
    > _"The meaning of a name is the object it denotes."_ (Tractatus 3.203)
    
- **Later Wittgenstein (Philosophical Investigations)**:
    
    > Ostensive definitions are **inherently ambiguous**—pointing alone cannot fix meaning without pre-existing linguistic context.
    
    ### **论证演变：中英双语分步解析**

#### **1. 《逻辑哲学论》的理想化模型**

- **英文**：
    
    > **Thesis**: Language mirrors reality via atomic facts composed of simple objects.
    > 
    > - Simple objects are **indefinable primitives** (e.g., "redness" as a basic quality).
    >     
    > - Their names acquire meaning **solely through ostension** ("This ⇒ RED").  
    >     → _Ostension bridges language and world._
    >     
    
- **中文**：
    
    > **核心命题**：语言通过由简单对象构成的原子事实镜像反映现实。
    > 
    > - 简单对象是**不可定义的初始实体**（如“红色”作为基本性质）。
    >     
    > - 其名称意义**仅通过实指获得**（“这 ⇒ 红”）。  
    >     → _实指定义连接语言与世界。_
    >     
    

#### **2. 后期批判：实指定义的模糊性**

- **英文**：
    
    > **Problem**: Pointing is always ambiguous.
    > 
    > - If I point to a red apple and say "red", you might think I mean:  
    >     `apple` / `round` / `fruit` / `shiny`...
    >     
    > - To resolve ambiguity, I must add words:  
    >     _"This **color** is ‘red’"_ → But this **presupposes** you understand "color"!  
    >     → _Ostension alone fails; it depends on prior language-games._
    >     
    
- **中文**：
    
    > **批判**：指向行为本质模糊。
    > 
    > - 若我指向红苹果说“红”，你可能理解为：  
    >     `苹果` / `圆形` / `水果` / `光泽`...
    >     
    > - 为消除歧义，需补充说明：  
    >     _“这种**颜色**叫‘红’”_ → 但这**预设**你已理解“颜色”一词！  
    >     → _纯粹实指定义失效；其意义依赖先存的语言游戏。_
    >     
    

#### **3. 根本转向：从指称到使用**

- **英文**：
    
    > **Key Insight**: Meaning is determined by **use in a form of life**, not by pointing.
    > 
    > - Example: Teaching "pain":  
    >     Not by pointing to a sensation, but by **linking the word to behavior** (e.g., wincing + saying "ouch!").
    >     
    > - Ostension only works within **pre-existing practices** (e.g., knowing what "naming a color" means).
    >     
    
- **中文**：
    
    > **核心洞见**：意义由**生活形式中的使用**决定，而非指向。
    > 
    > - 例：教授“疼痛”一词：  
    >     并非指向感觉，而是**将词语与行为关联**（如皱眉 + 喊“哎哟！”）。
    >     
    > - 实指定义仅在**先存实践**中有效（例如已知“命名颜色”的规则）。

- **英文**：
    
    > **"Meaning is use"**:
    > 
    > - Words/sentences gain meaning **only within concrete contexts** (language-games).
    >

7 implicatures
定义：Grice distinguishes, within the total signification, between what is said from what is implied(暗示的) or ‘implicated’.

Features of conversational implicatures：

They are 1) cancellable (explicitly or contextually), 2) non-detachable, and 3) can be explained in terms of the ‘conversational maxims.’

例子 *contextually cancellable*：
A: How innovative is Zhang San's thesis?  
B: He has cited many authoritative sources.  
Implicature: The thesis lacks original viewpoints. (Suggesting the deficiency by "answering a different question")  
  
Situation 2 (Canceling Implication)  
  
A: Please describe the characteristics of Zhang San's thesis writing.  
B: He cited a lot of authoritative literature.


A: Is this painting of high artistic value?  
B: The frame is made of pure gold.  
Implicature: The artwork itself is mediocre. (The focus is shifted to the decoration.)  
  
Situation 2 (Canceling Implication)  
  
A: Which paintings in the exhibition used precious materials?  
B: The frame of this painting is made of pure gold.

*Non-detachability*：
### **不可分离性的定义与问题**

#### **格赖斯原定义（Grice, p.43）**

- **英文**:
    
    > An implicature is **non-detachable** iff it is impossible to rephrase the utterance (saying ~the same thing) _without_ losing the implicature.
    
    - **Example**:
        
        > _A: Are you going to the party?_  
        > _B₁: I don’t like parties._ → **Implicature**: _No, I won’t go._  
        > _B₂: I’m not into parties._ → **Same implicature**: _No, I won’t go._  
        > → Implicature persists across paraphrases → **Non-detachable**.
        
- **中文**:
    
    > 含义具有**不可分离性**，当且仅当无法用其他方式表达相同内容却消除该含义。
    
    - **案例**:
        
        > _A: 你今晚去聚会吗？_  
        > _B₁: 我不喜欢聚会。_ → **含义**：_我不会去。_  
        > _B₂: 我对聚会没兴趣。_ → **相同含义**：_我不会去。_  
        > → 含义在改写中保留 → **不可分离**。
        

#### **核心缺陷：不可分离性无法区分含义与蕴涵**

- **英文论证**:
    
    > **Entailments are ALSO non-detachable!**
    > 
    > - _X is a father._ **ENTAILS** _X is male._
    >     
    > - _X is a dad._ **ENTAILS** _X is male._  
    >     → The entailment ("male") remains across synonyms → **Fails to distinguish implicature from entailment**.
    >     
    
- **中文论证**:
    
    > **蕴涵（entailment）同样不可分离！**
    > 
    > - _X是父亲。_ **蕴涵** _X是男性。_
    >     
    > - _X是爸爸。_ **蕴涵** _X是男性。_  
    >     → 蕴涵（“男性”）在同义改写中保留 → **无法区分含义与蕴涵**。
    >     
    

---

### **关键对比：含义 vs. 蕴涵的不可分离性**

|**特征**|会话含义（Implicature）|蕴涵（Entailment）|
|---|---|---|
|**不可分离性**|✔️ 改写后含义仍保留  <br>（例：聚会拒绝）|✔️ 改写后蕴涵仍保留  <br>（例：父亲→男性）|
|**可取消性**|✔️ 可被语境取消  <br>（例：_"我不喜欢聚会，但今晚会破例"_）|✖️ **不可取消**  <br>（例：_"X是父亲但不是男性"_ → 逻辑矛盾）|
|**来源**|合作原则的违反（如相关性准则）|语义真值条件（逻辑必然性）|

> **结论**：
> 
> - 不可分离性**不是会话含义的可靠诊断特征**（因蕴涵也具备该属性）。
>     
> - **可取消性（cancellability）** 才是关键区别标准：
>     
>     - 含义可取消（如添加"但我会去"）
>         
>     - 蕴涵不可取消（取消则导致矛盾）


7.	Grice’s conversational maxims 

In a conversation, people assume that the other speaker is ‘being co-operative’ or abiding by the ‘co-operative principle’. One does this by following the conversational maxims. These maxims help make conversation ‘a maximally effective exchange of information’ (p. 28).

Maxim of quantity 1: Make your contribution as informative(翔实的) as is required.

Maxim of quantity 2: Do not make your contribution more informative than is required.

Maxim of quality 1: Do not say what you believe is false.

Maxim of quality 2: Do not say something for which you lack adequate evidence.

Maxim of relation: Be relevant.

Maxim of manner: Be perspicuous (that is, clear), which includes,

•Avoid obscurity(朦胧) of expression.

•Avoid ambiguity(歧义).

•Be brief (avoid unnecessary wordiness).

•Be orderly(整齐的).

**数量准则**

  

1. 使你的话语提供的信息**如所需般翔实**。
2. 不要使你的话语提供的信息**超出所需**。

  

**质量准则**

  

1. 不要说自己认为虚假的内容。
2. 不要说缺乏充分证据的内容。

  

**关联准则**  
话语要**切题**。

  

**方式准则**  
话语要**清晰明了**（即表达清楚），具体包括：

  

- 避免表达**晦涩**；
- 避免产生**歧义**；
- 要**简洁**（避免不必要的冗长）；
- 要**有条理**。

### **核心问题：承诺是描述还是行动？**

#### **传统误解：承诺是心理描述**

- **假设**：
    
    > “I promise to write to you”（我承诺给你写信） ≈ “I intend to write to you”（我打算给你写信）  
    > → 若无意写信，则陈述为假（false）。
    
- **错误根源**：  
    将语言简化为**描述事实的工具**（如“雪是白的”），忽视其**建构社会现实**的功能。
    

#### **奥斯汀的颠覆：承诺是行动**

- **关键论证**：
    
    > 当说出 “I promise” 时：
    > 
    > - **不是描述意图**，而是**完成承诺行为**（如同举手宣誓）。
    >     
    > - 即使撒谎（无写信意图），承诺**仍已发生** → 只是“不真诚”（insincere），而非“虚假”（false）。
    >     
    
- **中文类比**：
    
    > 在婚礼上说“我愿意”——即使内心反悔，**法律上已婚**；问题在“失信”，不在“说假话”。

### **第一人称/第三人称不对称性（Asymmetry）**

|**句式**|性质|示例|分析|
|---|---|---|---|

### **施为句的“不恰当性”（Infelicity）六类**

### **施为句与祈使句的关联**

#### **形式像祈使，本质是施为**

- **案例**：
    
    > “I order you to charge!”（我命令你冲锋！）
    > 
    > - 表面：祈使句（命令行动）
    >     
    > - 实质：**完成“下令”行为**（非描述命令）

- **中文对照**：

#### **关键区别**

||施为句|祈使句|
|---|---|---|
|**功能**|完成行动（如承诺/命令）|要求他人行动|
|**语法标志**|第一人称 + 行事动词（promise/order）|动词原形（Charge!/冲锋！）|

### **施为句的形式标记（Linguistic Markers）**

#### **显性标志词 “hereby”（特此）**

- **功能**：  
    明示“**此言即行动**”，排除歧义。
    
- **中英案例**：
    
    > 英文：_"I **hereby** declare this bridge open."_

- **无标志词仍有效**：
