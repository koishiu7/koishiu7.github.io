### 英文文档正文翻译：

**“REALITY AND THOUGHT” *Mind*, 1888**
**《实在与思想》**，《心灵》（Mind）杂志，1888年

**Quick summary:** argue that thought and reality must be different. Then argue that the distinction between thought and reality does not commit one to Kantian unknowable things in the themselves. The reason is Monism, for it shows how the Absolute (reality) can be different from thought and yet not a Kantian unknowable.
**快速总结：**论证思想与实在必定是不同的。然后论证，思想与实在之间的区分并不必然使人陷入（承诺）康德式的不可知的“自在之物”。其理由是一元论，因为它展示了绝对（实在）如何能够不同于思想，却又不是康德式的不可知之物。

**I. Reality is not to be identified with Thought:**
**一、实在不可等同于思想：**

If we take any judgment we find the distinction of reality and idea. Fact and truth are not the same. Truth and thought are not the thing, but are of it and about it. Thought predicates an ideal content of a subject. The idea is not the same as fact, for in its existence and meaning are necessarily divorced. And the subject is neither the mere “what” of the predicate, nor any other mere “what”. Nor, if it is proposed to take up a whole with both its aspects, and to predicate the ideal character of its own subject, will that proposal lead us further. If there the subject is the same as the predicate, why trouble oneself to judge? If it is not the same, then what is it? And you are fast in the dilemma. There is no judgment at all, there is a pretence of thinking without thought; or there is a judgment, but the subject is more than the predicate. It is a “that” beyond the mere “what”. (371)
如果我们考察任何判断，就会发现实在与观念的区分。事实与真理并不等同。真理与思想并不是事物本身，而是属于事物且关于事物的。思想将观念的内容述谓给一个主体。观念与事实并不相同，因为在其存在与意义上是必然分离的。而且主体既不仅仅是谓词的“什么”（what），也不是任何其他纯粹的“什么”。并且，如果提议将其两个方面作为一个整体来把握，并述谓其自身主体的观念性特征，这一提议也不会引导我们更进一步。如果在那里主体与谓词相同，又何必费心去判断呢？如果它们不同，那又是什么呢？于是你就陷入了两难困境。要么根本没有判断，只是一种没有思想的思考的伪装；要么有判断，但主体多于谓词。它是一个超越纯粹“什么”的“那个”（that）。（第371页）
